# Online Retail Sales Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analysing online retail sales data, identifying business trends, and presenting actionable insights through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses an online retail dataset to understand sales performance, customer behaviour, product performance, and revenue trends.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow:

**Data Cleaning → SQL Analysis → Data Visualization → Dashboard → Business Insights & Recommendations**

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python** — Data cleaning and preprocessing
- **Excel** — Data validation and preparation
- **MySQL / SQL** — Data analysis and business queries
- **Tableau** — Interactive dashboard and visualization

## 📊 Dashboard

The Tableau dashboard provides an interactive view of:

- Total Revenue
- Total Customers
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Total Quantity Sold
- Revenue by Category
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top Products by Revenue
- Revenue by City

### Interactive Filters

- Month
- Quarter
- Category
- City
- Gender

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Electronics** was the top-performing category, generating approximately **$166.51K (22.58%)** of total revenue.
- **August** recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately **$79.07K**, while February recorded the lowest.
- **Smartphone** was the highest-revenue individual product at approximately **$38.32K**.
- **Cash on Delivery** was the most-used payment method, accounting for **374 transactions** and approximately **$274.38K** in revenue.
- **Q3** was the strongest-performing quarter, generating approximately **$206.33K** in revenue.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory and marketing for high-performing categories such as Electronics.
- Use top-performing products for bundles, upselling, and cross-selling opportunities.
- Run targeted promotional campaigns during lower-performing periods.
- Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of stronger periods such as Q3.
- Continue supporting Cash on Delivery while improving the convenience of digital payment options.
- Maintain a diversified product portfolio to reduce dependence on a single category or product.
